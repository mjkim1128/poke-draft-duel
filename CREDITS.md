# Credits

## Animations
- **Move, weather, terrain, status condition and stat change animations**: **The Gen 9 Move Animation Project** by KRLW890
  (https://www.pokecommunity.com/threads/the-gen-9-move-animation-project.526189/, https://eeveeexpo.com/resources/1480/)
  and the contributors of the **Gen 8 Move Animation Project** it builds on.
  - "Thanks to the Reborn team for letting people use their resources."
  - The animation data format and playback rules follow **Pokémon Essentials** (Maruno, Poccil and contributors).
  - Converted by `web/tools/pb/convert-pb-anims.py` into `web/public/pbanim`
    (data from `PkmnAnimations.rxdata`, pictures from `Graphics/Animations`). Their sound effects are not used.
  - Move animations play at twice their original speed.
